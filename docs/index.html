Momentum, ranked by return per unit of risk

A rules-based screener for NSE equities: rank the point-in-time NIFTY 500 by 12-month return (skipping the last month) divided by volatility, hold the top 25 equally, rebalance monthly. Backtested over 8+ years against the index and against a few hundred random portfolios drawn from the same universe — because in a mid-cap-heavy market, beating the index is a much lower bar than it sounds.

The honest benchmark

An equal-weight basket of randomly chosen stocks from this universe beats the cap-weighted NIFTY 500 most of the time — small and mid caps outran large caps over this window, and equal weighting leans into them. So "it beat the index" proves very little. The real test is where the ruleset lands inside the distribution of random portfolios that passed the same liquidity and trend filters.

What this null does and doesn't test. The random portfolios are drawn from names that already passed every filter — above the 200-day average, liquid enough, no spike — and only the ranking is randomised. So this measures what the momentum score adds on top of the filters, which is the strict reading. It does not credit the score for the filters' own contribution; the trend filter alone is doing real work, and the "raw 12-1, no vol divisor" row in the robustness table is what isolates the volatility divisor.
